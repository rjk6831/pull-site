Support

Questions, bugs or a purchase problem? Email solutions@rootsnolimits.com with your device model and the app version (shown at the bottom of Settings in PULL). We usually reply within two working days.

Restore purchases

Open Settings in PULL and tap Restore purchases. Remove Ads and the Starter Pack come back on any device signed in to the same Apple ID or Google account.

Refunds

Refunds are handled by Apple (reportaproblem.apple.com) or Google Play (play.google.com, Order history).
